# Next hypothesis: response aligned to a verified filing clock

The tested fixed ten-session momentum confirmation can include days before
or long after the relevant financial publication. A next deterministic-rule
experiment should measure the observed stock and Nifty response from the last
close before the verified financial-source clock to the decision close. It
must retain the original 31st-close checkpoint and subsequent 30-session
target, all 132 later cases, and pre-2025 parameter selection.

This is a testable hypothesis, not a proven edge. A recent primary study,
Christensen, Timmermann and Veliyev, *Warp speed price moves: Jumps after
earnings announcements* (author version revised 15 January 2026), reports
rapid announcement-related jumps and post-announcement strategy returns
consistent with efficient price formation after 2016. It does not validate
Indian IPO decisions or our model. Author source:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08962v2
Related journal DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2025.104010

The selected XBRL publication clock is a conservative availability reference,
not necessarily the first public earnings release. An original PDF or earlier
press release may precede it. Daily close-to-close changes do not isolate an
intraday announcement jump. Missing pre-publication prices must stay missing;
issue price or current-day prices cannot substitute for an unavailable close.
No published literature result should be copied as this application's score.
