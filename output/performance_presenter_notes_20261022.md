# Performance page — presenter notes

## What Module 1 supports

On the same 179 exchange-verified 2025–2026 outcomes, the retained three-input logistic model has AUC 0.814. At its 82% APPLY threshold, precision is 90.0% and overall policy accuracy is 73.2%, versus 67.0% for always predicting positive. The separate 50% direction cutoff has 76.0% accuracy. Do not interchange those metrics.

These are retrospective final-book results, not the accuracy of the application-time return model.

## Negative results

We reran 20 candidates on the same training and later cases. Later AUCs range from 0.793 to 0.819. No paired AUC improvement over the retained logistic model was established. Small differences alone do not prove equivalence.

Module 2: 132 matured cases, 47.0% 30-session direction accuracy, versus 58.3% always sell. It remains a transparent risk heuristic, not a validated forecast.

## How to discuss improvement honestly

An earlier-data-selected market-regime threshold experiment increased the sample score from 50.0% to 53.03% on the same 132 cases. It corrected 10 errors and introduced 6. Its change interval includes zero and it still loses to the baseline, so it was not promoted. This is research progress, not a proven live gain.

Split/bonus reconciliation, exchange-opening verification, stricter issuer/news matching and pre-close priority logs improve correctness and evidence. They must not be described as measured accuracy gains.

## Design challenges and next steps

Small sample, look-ahead leakage, missing/interim bids, capital actions, incomplete disclosures/news and retrospective evidence remain explicit. Next: genuine pre-cutoff prospective captures, calibrated probabilities, same-case significance tests, cost-aware monitoring and production access/storage/collector hardening. Do not claim formal ISO/IEEE conformance or invent a bibliography count.

The current application and full per-candidate ledger contain the detailed evidence. No paper title, paper body or model bundle was changed by this presentation work.
